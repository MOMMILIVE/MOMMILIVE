## Executive Summary

Mommi Live (born 28 August 1999 in Oslo, Norway) is a Norwegian creative director, film producer, author, and venture architect operating across high-fashion art direction, documentary cinema, and private venture architecture. As Founder and Managing Partner of **Mommi Ventures** (**Mommi Limited**), he orchestrates cross-industry mandates across Oslo, Milan, Paris, and Tbilisi. Previously, he founded the Nordic fine jewelry house **ICE FROM NORTH** in 2021, scaling global creator distribution (including collaborations with Khaby Lame) and earning editorial recognition in *Vogue Scandinavia* prior to exiting the company via private acquisition in Q4 2024.
